TaskRoulette

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

TaskRoulette ("we", "the app") is a simple task-picking tool: you dump your to-dos in, we sort them onto a spinning wheel, and it picks what you do next. This page explains what data we touch and why.

What we collect

  • Your task text. Anything you type into the brain-dump box, or any photo of a handwritten list you upload, is sent to Anthropic's API to be parsed into a task list. We don't store this text on our servers — it lives in your browser's local storage only.
  • Google Tasks (optional). If you choose to connect Google Tasks, we request read-only access (tasks.readonly) via Google OAuth (handled by Supabase Auth) so you can import existing tasks into TaskRoulette. We never write to, modify, or delete anything in your Google Tasks account. We only read task titles, due dates, and list names to show you a picker.
  • Email address (optional). If you sign in with Google or submit your email to keep using the app past the free session limit, we store your email (and first/last name, if provided by Google) in Loops, our email service provider, so we can send you occasional product updates. You can ask us to delete this at any time (see Contact below).
  • Basic usage counters. We track how many sessions you've completed today, keyed to your IP address, purely to enforce a free-tier usage limit. This resets daily and isn't tied to any other identifying information.

How we use Google user data

Google user data (specifically, your Google Tasks titles, due dates, and list names, obtained via the read-only tasks.readonly scope) is used solely to display those tasks to you inside TaskRoulette's task list and spinning wheel, so you can pick from your existing Google Tasks alongside anything you've typed or dumped in manually. We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, analytics, AI model training, or any purpose beyond showing it back to you in the app. Your Google OAuth access token and refresh token are used only to make read requests to the Google Tasks API on your behalf — never to log in elsewhere, never to access any other Google product or scope, and never to modify or delete anything in your Google account.

TaskRoulette's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who we share Google user data with

We do not sell, rent, or share your Google user data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party for their own purposes. Google user data passes through the following parties, and only as strictly necessary to make the feature work:

  • Google — the source of the data. Our backend server calls Google's own token endpoint (oauth2.googleapis.com) to exchange your refresh token for a fresh access token when the short-lived one expires. No Google data is sent anywhere else during this exchange.
  • Supabase Auth — brokers the OAuth login handshake itself (redirecting you to Google and back) and briefly holds your Google session token client-side so the app can call the Google Tasks API. Supabase does not receive your Google Tasks content.
  • Our own backend server (Hono, hosted on Vercel) — holds the Google OAuth client secret needed to refresh your access token, and proxies that one refresh request to Google. It does not read, log, or store your Google Tasks content, and does not persist your refresh token — that stays in your browser only (see Data retention below).

We do not send Google Tasks content to Anthropic (Claude) or to Loops. Anthropic only ever sees the free-text brain dump or photo you type/upload yourself — never your imported Google Tasks. Loops only ever receives an email address and name, never task content.

Data protection for sensitive data

All communication between your browser, our server, Supabase, and Google happens over HTTPS/TLS — Google user data is never sent in plaintext over the network. Your Google OAuth access token lives only in your browser's memory for the length of your session and is never written to disk or sent to our server. Your Google OAuth refresh token is stored in your browser's local storage only — it is never stored in a database on our servers. The Google OAuth client secret (needed to mint fresh access tokens) is kept exclusively as a server-side environment variable and is never exposed to the browser, logged, or committed to source control. Our backend does not log Google Tasks content or OAuth tokens.

Retention and deletion of Google user data

  • Access tokens (~1 hour lifespan, issued by Google) live only in browser memory and are discarded automatically when the tab closes or the token expires — we never persist them.
  • Refresh tokens are kept in your browser's local storage only for as long as you stay connected. They are deleted immediately when you disconnect Google Tasks from within the app, sign out, or revoke access from your Google account permissions page (which also invalidates the token on Google's side, so it can no longer be used even if a copy somehow remained).
  • Task titles/due dates/list names fetched from Google Tasks are held in browser memory to render the picker and are not persisted by us beyond your session, unless you manually add one to your TaskRoulette list — at which point it's stored the same way any manually-typed task is (in your browser's local storage), until you delete it or clear your browser data.
  • You can request full deletion of any data we do hold (your email in Loops) at any time — see Contact below. Since we don't store Google Tasks content or tokens on our servers, there is nothing server-side for us to delete on that front — disconnecting in the app or from your Google account permissions page fully removes it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use your task content for anything other than parsing it into a list for you.
  • We don't share your Google account data with any third party except Google itself (to refresh your access token) and Supabase (to handle the OAuth login flow itself) — see "Who we share Google user data with" above for detail.

Where your data lives

Your task list, wheel state, and completion history are stored in your browser's local storage — not on our servers. Clearing your browser data or using a different device/browser will start you fresh. Your email (if submitted) is stored in Loops. Your Google OAuth session is managed by Supabase Auth, and your Google refresh token is stored in your browser's local storage only (see above).

Third-party services we use

  • Anthropic (Claude) — parses your brain-dump text/photos into a task list. Never receives your Google Tasks content.
  • Google Tasks API — read-only task import, only if you opt in.
  • Supabase Auth — handles the Google OAuth login flow.
  • Loops — email delivery for product updates, only if you submit your email. Never receives your Google Tasks content.
  • Vercel — hosting.

Your choices

You can disconnect Google Tasks access at any time from within the app, or from your Google account permissions page — either immediately deletes your locally-stored refresh token and revokes our ability to access your Google Tasks. You can clear all locally stored task data from within the app, or by clearing your browser's site data for taskroulette.vercel.app.
