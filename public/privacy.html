TaskRoulette

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

TaskRoulette ("we", "the app") is a simple task-picking tool: you dump your to-dos in, we sort them onto a spinning wheel, and it picks what you do next. This page explains what data we touch and why.

What we collect

  • Your task text. Anything you type into the brain-dump box, or any photo of a handwritten list you upload, is sent to Anthropic's API to be parsed into a task list. We don't store this text on our servers — it lives in your browser's local storage only.
  • Google Tasks (optional). If you choose to connect Google Tasks, we request read-only access (tasks.readonly) via Google OAuth (handled by Supabase Auth) so you can import existing tasks into TaskRoulette. We never write to, modify, or delete anything in your Google Tasks account. We only read task titles and due dates to show you a picker.
  • Email address (optional). If you sign in with Google or submit your email to keep using the app past the free session limit, we store your email (and first/last name, if provided by Google) in Loops, our email service provider, so we can send you occasional product updates. You can ask us to delete this at any time (see Contact below).
  • Basic usage counters. We track how many sessions you've completed today, keyed to your IP address, purely to enforce a free-tier usage limit. This resets daily and isn't tied to any other identifying information.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use your task content for anything other than parsing it into a list for you.
  • We don't share your Google account data with any third party except Anthropic (to parse text/photos you submit) and Supabase (to handle the OAuth login flow itself).

Where your data lives

Your task list, wheel state, and completion history are stored in your browser's local storage — not on our servers. Clearing your browser data or using a different device/browser will start you fresh. Your email (if submitted) is stored in Loops. Your Google OAuth session is managed by Supabase Auth.

Third-party services we use

  • Anthropic (Claude) — parses your brain-dump text/photos into a task list.
  • Google Tasks API — read-only task import, only if you opt in.
  • Supabase Auth — handles the Google OAuth login flow.
  • Loops — email delivery for product updates, only if you submit your email.
  • Vercel — hosting.

Your choices

You can disconnect Google Tasks access at any time from your Google account permissions page. You can clear all locally stored task data from within the app, or by clearing your browser's site data for taskroulette.vercel.app.
